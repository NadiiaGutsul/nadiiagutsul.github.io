Home(less)

This comic was created as part of the course "Telling and Drawing Comics" during my Media Informatics studies at Hochschule Flensburg.

The story explores the experience of fleeing war and the search for a new home and identity.

The moment I heard the themes "homeless" or "restless," I knew I would tell my own story. First, because it's important to speak about what you truly know. Second, because this is something that directly affects me — and I felt this comic could serve an important purpose: to show people in peaceful countries how it feels to be forced to flee from war.

I included personal symbols and references to people, places, and things I lost, turning this comic into a space of memory, mourning, and quiet resistance.
