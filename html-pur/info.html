About Me

Hello! I am Lykho, a Media Informatics student at Hochschule Flensburg passionate about digital art, design, and tech. My creative journey started with drawing and expanded into 3D, animation, and game dev.

I use tools like Blender, ZBrush, Photoshop, Procreate, Unity, and have programming experience with Java and Android. I've created 3D characters, illustrations, animations, and game mechanics in both university and personal projects.

I'm passionate about character development—combining artistic vision and technical execution in concepts, models, and animations.

I love experimenting, collaborating, and improving my skills. My goal is to grow as a media designer who creates meaningful, emotionally engaging content.
