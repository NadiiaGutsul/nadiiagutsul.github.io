Creativity

This comic was created as part of the course "Creative Techniques" during my Media Informatics studies at Hochschule Flensburg.

    We were assigned to create our creative profile and answer the following questions:

  • What does creativity mean to me?
  • What is my most creative achievement?
  • What helps me to be creative?
  • Who is a person that serves as an example of creativity for me?

You can see the results of this in my comic.
